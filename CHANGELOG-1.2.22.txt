1025Dex 1.2.22 — Buyable evolution apples

Add Tart Apple and Sweet Apple, each priced at 2,100, to all eleven games.
Use them on Applin to evolve into Flapple and Appletun respectively.
These items do not evolve Dipplin. Applin's existing Dipplin branch is kept.

Red/Blue/Yellow and FireRed/LeafGreen: Celadon Department Store, 4F.
Gold/Silver/Crystal: Celadon Department Store, 4F; also Goldenrod's 4F.
Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald: Pretty Petal Flower Shop on Route 104, left-hand owner.
The other two flower-shop NPCs retain their Wailmer Pail and berry rewards.

Use native item registries, shop transactions, bag storage and evolution
flows. Gen 3 item IDs are stable across ROM reloads; replace Applin's old
Flapple/Appletun level-36 fallback with real apple-use branches. Purchased
apples work in FRLG before the National Dex unlock, while Everstone still
prevents their use. No other evolution's postgame restrictions are changed.

Tested purchases and item evolution/refusal in all eleven game fixtures on
engines 0.3.54 and 0.3.58, plus Gen 3 item reload and evolution selection.
The all-species evolution-animation and alternate-form regressions pass.
Graphical playthroughs are not part of these headless checks.

Install the complete 1025Dex folder and restart. CodeBreaker 0.7.2 remains
compatible and needs no new build for these items.
