1025Dex 1.1.19

- Repairs 39 imported species with no level-1 moves in the Gen-1-filtered
  source data, including the reported Fomantis (internal slot 817, level 4)
  and Blipbug (slot 888, level 5) crashes.
- Restores actual starting moves where FireRed supports them, matching ROM
  move-name spelling. Fomantis regains Fury Cutter.
- Where no starting move is supported, uses a compatibility substitute:
  Leech Life for Blipbug/Snom, Tackle otherwise. These are compatibility
  choices, not claims about canonical learnsets. Existing later moves remain.
- Separates land encounters from water/fishing. Aquatic-only species such as
  Magikarp, Goldeen, Feebas and Arrokuda no longer enter Route 6 grass pools.
  Amphibious species remain eligible on land; assigned map homes are preserved.
- Preserves the explicit exact-encounter override used by other mods.

Install: replace the previous 1025Dex package; do not enable both versions.
Validation: headless Lua regressions for all 639 imported species at levels
1-100, all 58 authored maps across 17 generation choices and 3 terrain modes,
encounter bridge wrappers, and actual engine schema conversion of both
reported species' starting moves. Not play-tested inside the launcher.
