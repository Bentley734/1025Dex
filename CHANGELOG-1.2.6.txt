1025Dex v1.2.6 — Evolution repair for FireRed, LeafGreen and Emerald

- Restore evolution rows after the initial content merge and late ROM-reload
  registry writes. Extended species no longer lose their evolution tables.
- Preserve shipped normal evolution levels, including overdue level-up offers.
- Preserve plain trade evolutions at level 36 and native stone evolutions.
- Preserve the held-item alternatives, their branch priority and consumption
  only after completed evolution. Cancellation keeps the held item.
- Fix numeric gender checks and load missing gender ratios for those checks.
- Enable Mantyke's Remoraid requirement and Pancham's Dark-type party check.
- Read Beauty from the native contest field.
- Moves unavailable in Gen 3 use the existing level-36 special-method fallback
  instead of requiring an impossible move.
- Preserve gender requirements on adapted Gallade/Froslass branches and give
  them priority over the ordinary branch when eligible. Both sexes of Espurr
  evolve into the pack's base Meowstic slot.
- Keep both Cosmoem branches reachable at level 53: Attack > Defense selects
  Solgaleo; Attack <= Defense selects Lunala.

See EVOLUTION-RULES.txt for the retained no-trade/held-item rules.
