1025Dex v1.1.27

- Slowpoke now evolves into Slowbro on any level-up at level 37 or higher.
- A Slowpoke holding King's Rock at level 37 or higher evolves into Slowking
  instead, with the held-item branch taking priority over Slowbro.
- King's Rock is consumed only after Slowking evolution completes; canceling
  the evolution preserves both Slowpoke and its held item.
- The reusable held-item level-branch rule is isolated in gen3evolutions.lua.
- Fixed conditional evolution guards to identify the evolving source Pokemon
  from the mon instead of mistaking the candidate target for the source.
- No learnsets or unrelated evolution records were changed.
