How to read this guide
  • Canon identifies a species in that game's native encounter tables for that exact area and method. Native residents keep their ROM level ranges; selected generations still apply.
  • Levels show the available candidate range. The native encounter roll determines the actual level. Fishing and Rock Smash also filter candidates using that rolled level, so a listed species is not available with every rod or roll.
  • Common, rare, featured, special and Ultra Beast labels describe selection tiers, not a species' individual spawn percentage. Rare candidates share the ordinary 5% rare-tier roll; featured candidates share a 10% roll. Added special species share a 1% roll when ordinary candidates exist. Ultra Beasts share 0.1% in eligible areas or 1% in designated endgame hubs; both require League clear.
  • Diglett's Cave retains its fixed 95% Diglett / 5% Dugtrio slots. Dugtrio is level 29 or 31. Route 1's featured Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle and Pikachu, plus Diglett's Cave residents, remain available under every generation setting.
  • Native scripted encounters, gifts, trades and roamers are outside these wild replacement pools. Story progression still controls access to areas. “League cleared” controls the added postgame pools; it does not bypass story access.
  • Use the Hoennto guide while the combined campaign mod is enabled. The FR/LG and Emerald guides describe standalone 1025Dex. These guides were generated from production Lua data and validated automatically; no live playthrough was performed.
