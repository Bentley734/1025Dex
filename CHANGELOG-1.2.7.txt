1025Dex 1.2.7 — Ultra Beast encounter policy

All eleven Ultra Beasts now use a separate League-gated pool, never ordinary common/rare or fallback pools. No random Ultra Beast spawns before clearing the Pokemon League, and none in beginner areas (including Viridian Forest), even after clearing it.

After League clear:
- Suitable habitat-matched land areas with a level ceiling of at least 45: shared 0.1% Ultra Beast roll; level 45–60, capped by the area's ceiling.
- Designated end-game hubs: shared 1% Ultra Beast roll; level 55–70. All eleven are available subject to WILD GENS.
  FR/LG: Victory Road floors, Cerulean Cave floors and Sevault Canyon.
  Emerald: Victory Road floors, Sky Pillar encounter floors and Artisan Cave floors.
- No added Ultra Beast fishing, water or Rock Smash pools.
- Chances apply to the combined Ultra Beast group, not separately per species. A failed roll never guarantees an Ultra Beast when ordinary replacement candidates are absent.

DexNav reads the same League-gated species and level ranges; targeted searches follow DexNav's existing search behavior. WildFollowers calls the same random policy, preserving rarity and levels. Existing legendary/mythical homes, native static/scripted encounters, species numbering, shiny animation and v1.2.6 evolution rules are retained.

Validation: 305404 Ultra Beast assertions across maps, terrains, all 17 generation choices and League states; 50512 existing special-home assertions; 103993 WildFollowers/actual-policy compatibility assertions. Archive contents outside the documented edits are byte-preserved from v1.2.6, including the evolution repair.

Replace the previous 1025Dex ZIP and restart. No additional WildFollowers or DexNav update is required. Existing spawned overworld Pokemon will refresh with their normal respawn/map transition.
