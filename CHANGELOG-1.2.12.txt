1.2.12 — Canon encounters and habitat audit

Preserves all vanilla area/channel/species entries from the supplied USA
FireRed, LeafGreen and Emerald ROMs, including Emerald extra headers and
Route 119 Feebas. Route 3 Jigglypuff and Viridian Forest Pikachu are retained.
FR/LG version-specific native data is selected by the active game.

The campaign atlas no longer removes native encounters to enforce its
two-home limit or exclusive region assignment. Native species may overlap
regions; added species still receive small, habitat-compatible local pools.
Added aquatic species respect freshwater/sea habitats. Bombirdier is a land
species again. Missing native maps and exact encounter channels are covered.

Native level ranges reach both battles and public DexNav/follower APIs.
Generation settings still filter canon species, except the existing Route 1
featured starters and weighted Diglett/Dugtrio rules. Added special species
and Ultra Beasts retain their existing League gates and rarity rolls.

Replace the previous 1025Dex with this ZIP and restart the session. Current
WildFollowers and DexNav use the updated public API without another update.
EncounterAtlas.csv was rebuilt by game. See ENCOUNTER-AUDIT.md for measured
restoration counts. Tests are headless; no interactive playthrough was run.
