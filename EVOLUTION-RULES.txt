1025Dex 1.2.6 — Evolution compatibility rules (FR/LG and Emerald)

Normal level evolutions retain the levels in the shipped evolution graph.
Pokémon already above their required level are offered evolution on their next
level-up. Use a Rare Candy or gain a level in battle; loading does not force it.
Everstone and canceling evolution still work.

Plain trade evolutions: level up at level 36 or higher. This includes Kadabra,
Machoke, Graveler, Haunter, Boldore, Gurdurr, Karrablast, Shelmet, Phantump and
Pumpkaboo. No actual trade is required.

Retained held-item alternatives (level up with the item equipped):
  Slowpoke -> Slowking: King's Rock, level 37 or higher.
  Slowpoke -> Slowbro: level 37 or higher without King's Rock.
  Poliwhirl -> Politoed: King's Rock.
  Onix -> Steelix: Metal Coat.
  Scyther -> Scizor: Metal Coat.
  Seadra -> Kingdra: Dragon Scale.
  Porygon -> Porygon2: Up-Grade.
  Clamperl -> Huntail: Deep Sea Tooth.
  Clamperl -> Gorebyss: Deep Sea Scale.
The other listed held-item alternatives work on the next level-up, without a
level-36 minimum, preserving the existing mod rules. The held item is consumed
only after evolution completes. Canceling leaves the Pokémon and item intact.
Poliwhirl's Water Stone -> Poliwrath route remains available.

Native evolution stones retain their native use-item behavior. Methods/items
that Gen 3 cannot perform retain the pack's level-36 compatibility fallback;
this is a mod alternative, not a claim that those species naturally evolve
at level 36. Gender and supported friendship/party/move checks still apply.
When multiple unavailable special methods share a parent, the existing
Attack/Defense split selects their branches.

Cosmoem evolves at level 53: Attack > Defense gives Solgaleo;
Attack <= Defense gives Lunala, making both version-exclusive routes reachable.

Combee and Salandit require females at levels 21 and 33 respectively.
Mantyke requires Remoraid in the party. Pancham requires a Dark-type party
member at level 32. Both sexes of Espurr reach base Meowstic at level 25.
