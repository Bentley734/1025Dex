1025Dex 1.2.23 - Rare post-League forms

All 364 shipped Mega and alternate forms can spawn after entering the Hall
of Fame, at levels 55-70, with a 2% combined chance in designated endgame
areas. WILD GENS filters by the base Pokemon's generation. Aquatic forms
use water/fishing encounters.

Kanto: Cerulean Cave and Victory Road; Sevault Canyon in FireRed/LeafGreen.
Johto: Mt. Silver, Route 28, Dragon's Den B1F and Victory Road.
Hoenn: Victory Road, Sky Pillar, Meteor Falls B1F second room, and Emerald's
Artisan Cave.

Natural and visible wild encounters preserve the exact form identity.
Scripted encounters and exact CodeBreaker selections retain their behavior.
Evolution apples and prior evolution animation fixes remain included.

Install: replace the 1025Dex folder and restart. No save migration needed.
Download 1025Dex-v1.2.23.zip; GitHub's automatic source archives are not the
packaged mod build.

Validation: all eleven games, all 364 forms, pre/post-League gates,
generation filters, habitats, exact species identities, and 2% weights.
Live Gen 3 hooks checked on engine 0.3.54 and 0.3.58 using headless fixtures.
