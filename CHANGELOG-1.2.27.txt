1025Dex 1.2.27 - Adjustable EE playback

EE PAUSE: 1-7 seconds of rest after playback (default 7).
EE LOOPS: 1-5 complete animation loops before resting (default 1).
SPRITE SPEED controls the playback rate. The pause begins after the final
loop completes. Battle images and cached Gen 3 images use the same settings.

Selecting/reopening a Pokedex entry immediately restarts EE playback,
including returning to a previously cached species or selecting a form.
Repeated draws of the same selection do not restart it every frame.

Validation covers all 35 settings combinations, full pause durations,
repeated cycles, cached selection/reopening, and existing sprite regressions.

Download 1025Dex-v1.2.27.zip, replace the installed 1025Dex folder and restart.
No save migration required. Previous art and fixes included. GitHub's
automatic Source code archives are not the packaged mod build.
