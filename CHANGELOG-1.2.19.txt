1025Dex 1.2.19

SPRITE SIZE now offers BIG (default), NORMAL and SMALL. MAX DETAIL is removed.
Existing MAX DETAIL selections use the existing BIG rendering behavior.
Existing NORMAL and SMALL selections keep their sizes. Older pixel-height
settings retain their divisor conversion. Animated, Gen9 static and imported
BW artwork keep their shared animation transform and ground anchoring.
