1.2.31 — Full encounter randomizer

New mod option: Random encounters (all forms), default OFF.
Equal odds across 1,025 base Pokemon and 364 registered alternate forms.
Includes Megas, Gmax, Primals, regional forms and all other registered forms,
with no habitat, generation or League restrictions while enabled.
Preserves native encounter levels and failed encounter/fishing rolls.
Visible wilds and encounter-list providers share the same randomizer pool.
Disabling restores the existing native/curated/post-League encounter policy.
Scripted/trainer/roaming and exact selected battles are preserved.

Validation: schema-backed boot/registry/encounter tests on all eleven games;
all 1,389 identities reachable with one equal-weight draw; pre-League water,
visible wilds, battle handoff, encounter-list weights, OFF, and failed rolls.
