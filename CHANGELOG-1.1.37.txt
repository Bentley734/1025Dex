1025Dex v1.1.37

- Added Sevii encounter profiles for all seven islands, their outdoor routes,
  Mt. Ember, Berry Forest, Dunsparce Tunnel, Icefall Cave, Lost Cave,
  Pattern Bush, Altering Cave, Dotted Hole, Sevault Canyon and Tanoby Ruins.
- Island encounters use habitat-based generation-filtered pools and regional
  level ranges (30-46 on the early islands, up to 62 on Seven Island;
  individual caves/routes have their own ranges). Land and water stay separate.
- Recognizes FR_ and legacy SEVII_ map IDs, dungeon floors and map-catalog aliases.
- Unknown maps retain native encounters instead of borrowing Route 1's pool.
- Battle conversion keeps the encounter's source map. DexNav/WildFollowers
  receive the same island pools and level ranges through the existing export.
- PC expanded to 36 boxes x 30 slots = 1,080 stored Pokemon, excluding the party.
  Existing boxes, contents, names, wallpapers and PC items are preserved.
  Native capture spillover, box selection, deposit/withdraw/move and save/load
  use the expanded capacity automatically.

INSTALL
Replace the previous 1025Dex mod folder with this version; do not install a
second copy alongside it. Restart the game before loading your save.
Back up your save first. Keep this version or a compatible expanded-storage
version enabled when loading/saving Pokemon in boxes 15-36: the unmodified
engine only restores/saves 14 boxes. Do not downgrade or disable the mod while
those boxes hold Pokemon.

VALIDATION
Automated Lua checks against Gen1Recomp v0.3.20 storage/UI/serializer code:
1,632 Sevii location/generation/terrain combinations; map aliases and dungeon
floors; land/water restrictions; source-map battle conversion; scripted-battle
bypass; DexNav export; old-save expansion; capture through slot 1,080 and full-PC
rejection; deposit/withdraw/move; box 14/15 navigation and 36/1 wrap; dense and
sparse save serialization/restoration including the last slot.
A live in-game playtest was not available.
