1025Dex 1.2.32 fixes compatibility with Gen1Recomp’s save editor and expanded PC boxes.

- The Gen 3 editor now lists all 1,025 base Pokémon and 364 alternate forms, with the same species slots used in-game.
- Expanded boxes survive opening and exporting from the editor, preserving Pokémon, sparse slots, names and wallpapers. Legacy box mirrors do not duplicate Pokémon.
- Mega, Gmax and regional form metadata survives Gen 3 registration.
- Gen 2 National Dex growth-curve names resolve to the native curves.
- National Dex 0.35.5 / Modern Spawns 0.9.0 GB saves retain existing species and form IDs, nicknames, trainer IDs and moves when switching to 1025Dex. No renumbering or save conversion is required.

Restart the game before opening the editor. Enable 1025Dex in place of National Dex; their existing conflict rule remains. Modern Spawns still requires its declared National Dex dependency. The supplied National Dex archive supports Gen 1/2; this release does not claim compatibility with an unprovided third-party Gen 3 National Dex implementation.

Validation: sandboxed editor boots across all eleven supported games; all 1,389 identities and 364 forms checked against the supplied archive; actual reference-mod scratch saves imported across all six GB games; editor/native-PC round trips and expanded-box preservation; existing all-game and encounter regressions. Tests use read-only local ROM caches and scratch saves, with graphics stubbed. No user saves are modified.
