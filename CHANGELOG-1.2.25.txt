1025Dex 1.2.25 - EE animation interval

EE ANIMATED plays one animation every seven seconds and holds its first pose
between animations. Animation speed still controls playback speed. Applies
to battles, Pokedex, summaries and cached Gen 3 preview images.
EE STATIC, DBK animated, BW ROM and party-icon timing retain their behavior.
Single-frame sprites stay still. All previous artwork and fixes are included.

Install: download 1025Dex-v1.2.25.zip, replace the installed 1025Dex folder
and restart. No save migration required. GitHub's automatic Source code
archives are not the packaged mod build.
