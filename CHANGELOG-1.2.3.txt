1025Dex 1.2.3

Automatic battle height correction for FR/LG and Emerald. The sprite baker
measures the complete animation's visible bounds and exposes its stable top
headroom. Battle positioning caps the existing 14px lift to that headroom.
Tall sprites move down only by the excess lift; smaller sprites retain their
previous coordinates. No species list or manually entered offsets are needed.

Armarouge front/back fill their 64px picture height and move down 14px.
Pikachu, Wooloo and Zigzagoon retain their existing placement in default
FEET mode. Each front/back sheet is measured separately. Animation frames
share one correction, preventing placement jitter. Floating species retain
their baked hover; only the extra blanket battle lift is constrained.

Battle bounce and other animation offsets remain additive. Double-battle
slots and transformed displayed species use their own picture metadata.
Native ghost/substitute/unmanaged pictures keep their previous behavior.
Art, image size, animation, menu previews and overworld sprite positions
are unchanged. All 1.2.2 encounters, including League-gated Mew, are retained.

Validation: production bake placement across all 64 art heights and the
production battle wrapper (144 checks); full provider compilation; bundled
Armarouge/Pikachu/Wooloo/Zigzagoon sheet bounds. Encounter/DexNav/follower
compatibility suites pass. A live in-game playtest was unavailable.
