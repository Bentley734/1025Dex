has 90 added special species plus four native statics; Emerald has 94 added

1025DexNav and WildFollowers use the same newly unlocked pool automatically.
Production-code tests cover League flags, all 17 generation selections,
FR/LG aliases, pre/post-League cache changes, and terrain/level restrictions.
WildFollowers compatibility tests pass. Full in-game playtesting unavailable.
