1025Dex 1.2.8 — October 3, 2026

FIRST CATCHES
- Emerald's caught-entry adapter now returns the full caught-mon packet to
  newer engines instead of invoking their callback with nil. It retains the
  animated picture, personality, OT IDs and shiny palette.
- Release the temporary caught portrait after its fade and before advancing
  to the nickname question or PC message. Normal battle text is visible again
  in FireRed, LeafGreen and Emerald, including with HD battle UI mods.
- Preserve NoNickname in both load orders, party/PC handling, duplicate catches
  and older engine return behavior. Capture rewards are never granted twice.

CAVE ENCOUNTERS (FIRERED / LEAFGREEN)
- Mt. Moon 1F: levels 7–10. B1F: 5–10. B2F: 8–12.
- Diglett's Cave uses its original twelve weighted slots: Diglett 95%, levels
  15–22; Dugtrio 4% at level 29 and 1% at level 31. No other species appear.
- This protected Diglett pool applies under every WILD GENS selection.
- Displaced ordinary residents retain catchable homes in Rock Tunnel rather
  than vanishing from the expanded Dex. High evolution gates stay out of Moon.
- Native random battles, WildFollowers and 1025DexNav share the updated policy.
  DexNav receives separate Diglett/Dugtrio level ranges and 95/5 slot weights.

GUIDES
- Rebuilt both offline HTML guides from the current encounter policy.
- Include corrected cave levels/rarity, relocated species and Ultra Beast
  League requirements, 1% hub pools and 0.1% eligible late-area pools.
- Every one of the 1,025 species has an encounter source in each guide.

Preserves previous evolution rules (including level-36 trades and held-item
branches), animated normal/shiny sprites, battle placement, storage and
postgame encounter fixes. WildFollowers 2.22.9 runtime code is unchanged.

Validation: production first/repeat catch flows for 0.3.43 and current engine,
FR/LG and Emerald, party/PC, normal/shiny and both NoNickname load orders;
exact cave weights across all 17 generation choices and both League states;
shared encounter APIs and current WildFollowers; special/Ultra Beast rules;
sprite animation/placement; HTML coverage and offline interaction checks.
An interactive game playthrough was not run.
