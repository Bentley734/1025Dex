1025Dex v1.1.28

- Added a reusable held-item level-up evolution method for FireRed/LeafGreen
  trade evolutions whose required held items already exist in the game.
- Slowpoke holding King's Rock evolves into Slowking at level 37 or later;
  otherwise its normal Slowbro evolution remains available.
- Added King's Rock Poliwhirl -> Politoed, Metal Coat Onix -> Steelix and
  Scyther -> Scizor, Dragon Scale Seadra -> Kingdra, Up-Grade Porygon ->
  Porygon2, and both Deep Sea item branches for Clamperl.
- Held items are consumed only after a successful evolution. Canceling an
  evolution leaves the item intact and permits another offer on a later level.
- Preserved Poliwhirl's Water Stone evolution and all unrelated evolution and
  moveset data.
